# Oldskaters — presenting portrait skate videos

How might Oldskaters present portrait videos filmed on phones during skate sessions so they retain the movement, spontaneity, and energy of skating, without feeling like flat video tiles in a web layout?

Start by examining the existing interaction at https://oldskaters.com: visitors begin with one skater, and tapping reveals more people skating together. This playful discovery expresses the feeling of joining a community. Build on that progression from skating alone to skating together while exploring how framing, composition, and transitions can improve the presentation of portrait footage across the web app.
